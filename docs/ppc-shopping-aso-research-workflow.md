# Choose an ad, product-price or Google Play research task

These Actors answer different research questions. Choose the one that matches your work instead of running them as a compulsory sequence.

| Your question | Guide | Actor report fee, USD |
|---|---|---|
| Which ad creatives did the provider return for this advertiser? | [Review Google Ads Transparency creatives](google-ads-transparency-competitor-review.md) | $0.04 per advertiser snapshot, up to 40 creatives. |
| Which priced offers were returned for this product query? | [Compare Google Shopping prices](google-shopping-price-comparison.md) | $0.08 per product-query report. |
| Which apps rank for this Google Play keyword? | [Inspect Google Play keyword results](google-play-aso-keyword-research.md) | $0.035 per app-keyword report. |

Live data requires a paid Apify plan, and applicable platform plan charges are separate from these report fees. Provider access is included. Events are reserved before requests, so errors or empty results can occur after billing.

The ad Actor returns an inventory with links for manual creative review; it does not reliably extract ad copy or compare runs. Shopping results require checking that the models and variants are comparable. Google Play scores do not establish search volume or likely installs, and the Actor does not cover Apple's App Store.

Each focused guide links a supplied input and explains the output fields. Start with one Actor, retain its source evidence and timestamp, then decide whether another question needs a separate paid run.
